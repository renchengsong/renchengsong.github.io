LTHRV

Video-based Pulse, Breathing, and Long-term HRV Analysis Dataset
Open →

A multimodal physiological dataset for long-term heart rate variability analysis, containing synchronized RGB video, PPG and respiration signals. Subjects were recorded in a supine position under two conditions: natural breathing, and apnea with timed breath-holds that widen the range of heart rate variability.

Subjects
18 (12 M / 6 F), aged 21–25
Video
Logitech C920, 30 fps, 640×480, uncompressed YUV
Reference
Contec CMS50EA oximeter + HKH-11C respiration sensor
Protocol
Natural breathing and apnea, 15 min each

PV3D

Physiological-assisted Visual Deception Detection Dataset
Open →

A multimodal deception detection dataset built around a simulated theft scenario, combining video, audio, heart rate, respiration, blood volume pulse and blood oxygen saturation. To our knowledge it is the first dataset that supports video deception detection, remote heart rate measurement and remote respiration measurement at the same time.

Subjects
101 participants — 48 deceptive, 53 truthful
Video
Logitech C920 HD Pro, 30 fps, 640×480, with audio
Reference
Contec CMS50E oximeter + HKH-11C respiration sensor
Size
Approx. 639 GB
Deception detectionrPPGMultimodal

BSIPL-motion

Biomedical Signal and Image Processing Laboratory Motion Scenario Dataset
Open →

A benchmark for motion-robust remote physiological measurement. Four motion scenarios — speaking, anterior–posterior movement, horizontal movement and random head rotation — are recorded with synchronized reference PPG under two lighting conditions.

Subjects
33 (26 M / 7 F), aged 21–25
Video
Logitech C920, 30 fps, 640×480, uncompressed YUV
Reference
Contec CMS50E oximeter, 60 Hz PPG
Scenarios
Speaking, anterior–posterior, horizontal, random rotation
Motion robustnessrPPGBenchmark

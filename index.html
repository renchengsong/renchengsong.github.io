Biography

I am a Professor at the School of Instrument Science and Opto-electronics Engineering, Hefei University of Technology. My research focuses on non-contact intelligent sensing and intelligent signal processing, with an emphasis on vision-based sensing, electromagnetic sensing, multimodal fusion, and physics-driven deep learning. By exploiting the complementary characteristics of visual and electromagnetic modalities, I investigate the non-contact sensing of both the apparent and the intrinsic properties of a target. My work centres on two fundamental challenges — response modelling and robust perception in complex environments — following a problem-driven paradigm that connects theoretical innovation, methodological development and real-world application.

I am looking for self-motivated master students interested in non-contact intelligent sensing, physics-driven deep learning, or biomedical measurement. Interested candidates are welcome to email me a resume.
